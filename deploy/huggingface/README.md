# Which Shinobi Are You?

Find out which Naruto character you look like, then train their jutsu with
real hand signs and cast them as 3D AR effects on your webcam.

Allow camera access when asked. Works best in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Source: https://github.com/MohamedTouaa/JutsuHandSigns

Susanoo models are CC-BY 4.0 (credits in `web/public/models/susanoo/CREDITS.md`
in the source repo); UI sounds credits in `web/public/sfx/CREDITS.md`.
